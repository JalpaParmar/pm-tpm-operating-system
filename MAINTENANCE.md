# Maintenance Guide

Monthly/before active use: review navigation and organization-specific status/severity/finance/metric definitions.

Quarterly: remove duplication, verify formulas in regenerated spreadsheets and refresh fast-changing technical/AI guidance.

After real projects: generalize learning only when confidentiality permits; never commit proprietary/customer data.

Update canonical guidance first, then regenerate affected binaries. AI may propose maintenance; humans verify logic/calculations/experience claims.
