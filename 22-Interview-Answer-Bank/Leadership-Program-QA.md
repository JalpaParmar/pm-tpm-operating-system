# Leadership / Program Q&A

## Influence without authority
Build shared outcome/evidence, understand constraints, make dependencies and decision rights explicit, and create visible operating mechanisms.

## Cross-team dependencies
Track provider/consumer, deliverable, needed-by vs forecast, criticality, mitigation and escalation trigger. Mock work does not close real integration.

## Executive communication
Lead with outcome/impact, confidence, material change, options and decision needed.

## Underperforming workstream
Clarify expected outcome/evidence, identify systemic/skill/dependency blockers, agree recovery checkpoints and escalate priority/capacity decisions appropriately.

## Governance without bureaucracy
Use the minimum cadence/artifacts that create decisions: milestones, dependencies, RAID, financial/quality signals and decision log.

## Improve predictability
Expose dependencies, reduce hidden work/churn, use historical evidence, shorten feedback loops and track forecast accuracy.

## Technical debt vs features
Translate debt into lead time, incident/security risk, blocked roadmap or toil; compare treatment with product value.
