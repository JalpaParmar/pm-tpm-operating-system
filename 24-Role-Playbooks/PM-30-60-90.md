# PM — 30/60/90 Days

**1–30 Understand:** outcomes, stakeholders, roadmap, delivery process, RAID, finances, quality/release and communication.  
**31–60 Stabilize:** baselines/ownership, dependency/risk visibility, useful governance/status, fix high-friction gaps.  
**61–90 Improve:** use trend evidence to improve predictability/flow/quality and document next-quarter improvements.
