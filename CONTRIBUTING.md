# Quality & Contribution Rules

1. Practical usefulness over template count.
2. Do not fabricate personal/project achievements or confidential data.
3. Label fictional examples.
4. Avoid duplicate templates that solve the same problem.
5. Link canonical definitions rather than copy them everywhere.
6. Metrics require definitions and interpretation guidance.
7. Formulas/calculations must be testable.
8. A blank template alone is insufficient: add instructions and examples.
9. Keep artifacts usable by PMs beyond one company/tool.
10. TPM material should connect technical risk to delivery decisions.
11. AI workflows must preserve human approval for consequential decisions.
12. Final artifacts should be readable on common desktop tools; Markdown navigation should also work well on mobile.
