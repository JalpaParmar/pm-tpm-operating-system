# Budget Forecasting

## Monthly cycle
Baseline → actuals → committed/remaining forecast → EAC → variance → cause → corrective options → stakeholder decision.

## Variance causes
Scope change, rate/resource mix, schedule extension, rework/quality, vendor/cloud cost, assumptions and timing/accounting differences.

## Cost-to-value
Pair financial view with intended outcome/status. A workstream under budget but not delivering value is not automatically healthy.

Do not invent precision. Record finance source/date and assumptions behind forecast.
