# PM / TPM Operating Model

```text
Initiate
  ↓
Plan & Baseline
  ↓
Execute & Coordinate
  ↓
Measure & Forecast
  ↓
Communicate & Decide
  ↓
Release / Transition
  ↓
Close & Learn
```

Risk, change, quality, stakeholder communication and technical governance run across the lifecycle.

## PM lens
Outcome, scope, schedule, resources, cost, risk, quality and stakeholders.

## TPM lens
All PM concerns plus technical dependencies, API/environment readiness, architecture risk, release/observability and engineering-quality signals.

## Tailoring
A two-week internal improvement does not need the same governance as a multi-team regulated program. Use the minimum operating structure that provides control and visibility.
