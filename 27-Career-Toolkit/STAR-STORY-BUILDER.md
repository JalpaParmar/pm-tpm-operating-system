# STAR Story Builder

Use only real experience.

**Situation:** context and stakes in 2–3 sentences.  
**Task:** your responsibility/decision.  
**Actions:** 2–4 specific things you did and why.  
**Result:** measurable/observable outcome; do not invent metrics.  
**Learning:** what changed in your later approach.

Build reusable stories for incident, dependency delay, release, scope change, stakeholder conflict, quality problem, automation improvement, technical decision and multi-team program.
