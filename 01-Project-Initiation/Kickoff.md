# Project Kickoff Agenda & Facilitation Guide

Purpose: create shared understanding of why the project exists, what is in/out, how decisions are made and what happens next.

| Time | Topic | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Why now / desired outcome | Sponsor |
| 10 min | Problem, objectives, success | Product / PM |
| 10 min | Scope in/out and assumptions | PM / TPM |
| 10 min | Approach, milestones, dependencies | PM / TPM / Engineering |
| 10 min | Roles, RACI, governance, communication | PM |
| 10 min | Top risks / open questions | All |
| 5 min | Decisions, actions, next checkpoint | PM |

Required outputs: charter approval path, scope questions, stakeholder/RACI updates, initial risks/dependencies, decisions/actions and next cadence.
