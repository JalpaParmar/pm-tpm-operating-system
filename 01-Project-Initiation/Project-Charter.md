# Project Charter Template

## Header
Project name | Sponsor | PM/TPM | Version/date | Draft/Approved.

## Sections
1. Problem / Opportunity — current problem, impact and evidence.
2. Objectives & Success Measures — outcomes, measures, owners/data sources; do not invent targets.
3. Scope — In — included capabilities/deliverables.
4. Scope — Out — explicit exclusions.
5. Deliverables & Milestones — deliverable, owner, milestone, acceptance evidence.
6. Stakeholders & Governance — decision/escalation cadence.
7. Assumptions & Constraints — assumptions to validate plus time/budget/technology/compliance/resource constraints.
8. Initial Risks & Dependencies — top initiation items only.
9. High-Level Approach — workstreams and technical integration/environment strategy where relevant.
10. Approval — approver, role, decision/date, conditions.

## Quality check
- [ ] problem/outcome clear
- [ ] in/out scope explicit
- [ ] success measures credible
- [ ] owners/governance known
- [ ] assumptions/risks/dependencies visible
- [ ] approval authority clear
