# PM / TPM Glossary

- **RAID:** Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Dependencies.
- **RACI:** Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed.
- **WBS:** Work Breakdown Structure.
- **Baseline:** approved reference for scope/schedule/cost comparison.
- **Critical path:** sequence of dependent activities that determines the earliest completion under the schedule model.
- **Risk:** uncertain event/condition that may affect objectives.
- **Issue:** problem that has already occurred and needs action.
- **Dependency:** relationship where progress/outcome relies on another deliverable/team/event.
- **Milestone:** significant point/event in the plan, usually zero duration in formal scheduling.
- **Variance:** difference between planned/baseline and actual/forecast.
- **Change request:** documented proposal to modify an approved baseline or material commitment.
- **RCA:** Root Cause Analysis.
- **RTO / RPO:** recovery time / recovery point objectives.
- **DORA metrics:** widely used software-delivery performance measures; definitions should be applied consistently.
