# Day 2 Roadmap — PM / TPM Operating System

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Repository structure, navigation, artifact standards, quality rules and roadmap.

## Phase 2 — Project Initiation
Project charter, business case/problem framing, objectives, scope, stakeholder register, RACI and kickoff.

## Phase 3 — Planning, Estimation & Resources
WBS, milestones, schedule, estimation, capacity/resource planning and baseline.

## Phase 4 — Agile Execution & Dependencies
Sprint/delivery practices, dependency management, critical path, execution cadence and blockers.

## Phase 5 — RAID + Change Management
RAID register, risk scoring, issue escalation, decision log, change request and scope governance.

## Phase 6 — Budget + Metrics + Dashboards
Budget/forecasting, variance, cost-to-value, delivery/flow/quality metrics and practical Excel dashboards.

## Phase 7 — Stakeholder Communication & Governance
Communication plan, weekly status, executive reporting and steering committee deck.

## Phase 8 — Release + Quality + Incident/RCA
Release readiness, go/no-go, rollout, quality gates, incident coordination and RCA.

## Phase 9 — AI-Assisted PM/TPM + Playbooks
Meeting/action extraction, status drafting, risk analysis, requirement structuring, incident synthesis and responsible AI workflows.

## Phase 10 — Complete Artifact Pack + Examples + Audit ✅
Master artifact catalog, end-to-end fictional project, project closure, quick start, maintenance guidance and final repository audit completed.

## Gate rule
Complete and review each phase before moving to the next.
